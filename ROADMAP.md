# Noxio roadmap

## Open
Nothing planned right now.

## Needs a check on a phone
Built and unit tested where there is logic, and looked at on an emulator, but not yet confirmed on a real device (the rest of the earlier list has been checked on a phone):
- Pause for other sounds with Element voice messages and other messengers, and that it never pauses between songs
- Tag editing: editing a few songs on a phone, and checking that other players read the changed tags
- Queue before the library: that after a restart the song that played is in the play strip and plays right away while the library still loads, and that the rest of the queue (also shuffled) is there afterwards without a gap in the music
- Short and square screens on the Clicks Communicator itself
- Keyboard phone mode on the Communicator: whether Automatic detects its keyboard, and whether Ctrl, Alt+number and Ctrl with comma or period arrive as expected

## Done
- Search shows the same artists as the Artists tab: with Hide secondary album artists or Hide song-only artists on, it no longer lists artists that have no albums and no songs there (unit tested). On the share cards' top lists a long name is written smaller first and only wraps to a second line when it still does not fit, like the name on the item card (unit tested). Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Credits: About > Licenses opens a list of Noxio, what it is built on (Auxio, Musikr, Media3, TagLib, FFmpeg) and every library it ships (AndroidX as one entry), each with its author and license; a tap shows the full license text, which is bundled. The list is written at build time by the AboutLibraries Gradle plugin, so it follows dependency updates; what Gradle cannot see and corrected licenses are in `config/aboutlibraries` (list logic unit tested). Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Pause for other sounds (Playback settings, on by default): Noxio pauses when another app starts playing a sound without asking Android for audio focus, like voice messages in Element, and plays on when it ends. It watches which players are playing, reacts only to a new one that lasts 0.7 seconds, leaves out short sounds and players that are only set up, and stops waiting once you play or pause yourself (unit tested). Apps that do ask for focus were already handled by Media3. Not tested on a phone yet.
- About: the name is centered with the icon to its left, a tap on Version opens the changelog (the Changelog row is gone) and Licenses no longer opens Auxio's wiki. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Filtering an artist or a playlist hides the page header (cover, name and details), so the matches show right away; it comes back when the field is empty. About no longer counts genres (they are left out of Noxio). The queue's search button sits as close to the playing strip as on the other screens (the queue no longer keeps room for its hidden search row), and the Shuffle all button closes when the tab or page changes. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Player and album pages: a tap on the song, artist or album in the player, or on the artist of an album page, no longer opens the album or artist (easily hit by mistake); their long press menus have Go to artist and Go to album. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Settings tab: the floating search button searches every setting (title and summary, ignoring case and accents; unit tested) and lists the matches above the search field with the screen they are on, the best one last; a tap opens that screen. The list sits right above the playing strip. On home a long press on the search button shows a Shuffle all button above it (a tap elsewhere closes it) instead of a small menu. Choose picture lists the albums from A to Z and is only in the menu of the Artists tab. A guide to all taps, long presses and swipes is in docs/GESTURES.md, linked from the README. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Settings is the last home tab (swipe past Playlists or tap the gear; no search row or shuffle button there); its rows open the same screens as before. The floating button is a search button and the search rows are gone: a tap opens the search (home, album) or shows the filter field above the playing strip (artist, playlist, queue), which hides again when empty; a long press opens Shuffle all on home and the page's menu elsewhere, which now starts with Shuffle. Page headers: a long press on the header of an album, artist or playlist page opens the same menu, which also has Sort; the sort and ⋮ buttons in the Songs header and the sort in the bottom row are gone, and the header text is centered. Sort choices: an artist's albums by name or date, an artist's songs always by name, an album by track or name (an album with several discs keeps its discs in order and sorts within each, unit tested), a playlist by name (that sort rewrites the playlist's order), the queue has the floating search button instead of its sort and ⋮ (a long press offers Shuffle queue, with the playing song staying, Save as playlist and Clear queue, also Ctrl+M); a stored sort that is no longer offered falls back to the default (unit tested). "Choose picture" in an artist's menu picks one of their album covers (albums without a cover are left out) instead of the collage, kept in the settings and so in the backup (unit tested). Buttons no longer show a hint on a long press. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Home without the menu: the bottom row is only the search field; the tabs show only a bigger icon (the label stays for screen readers, a long press shows no hint) with a Settings icon at their end (shown also with a single tab or in keyboard phone mode by touch); Settings lists Appearance, Library, Playback, Listening stats, Backup and About. The home lists are always sorted by name (a stored sort is ignored, unit tested); album, artist and playlist pages keep their sort, an artist's albums now start newest first. Artist and playlist pages lose their bottom three-dot menu (their actions are in the long press of the artist or playlist). The keyboard phone mode keeps Ctrl+M for that menu.
- Long presses instead of three dots: a song in the queue opens Remove from queue, Add to playlist, Go to artist, Go to album and View properties (the swipe to remove is gone; the right place is removed when a song is in the queue twice, unit tested); the song, artist and album in the player are centered and a long press on them opens the song's menu (a tap still goes to them), and so does a long press on the playing strip, which opened the album before. Share is gone from every menu (songs, the queue, the player and a selection). Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Backup files are named `Noxio_Backup_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS.zip`, so a second backup on the same day gets its own file instead of replacing the first; the last five files are kept, whatever their day. Files of earlier versions (`Noxio_YYYY-MM-DD.zip`, lower-case `noxio_`, `_small`) still count as backups from the start of their day, and an accepted smaller level is kept to the second. Unit tested; not tried on a device.
- Every file Noxio writes for the user follows one pattern, `Noxio_<What>_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS`: `Noxio_Stats_….csv` for the exported history, `Noxio_Artists_….png` (Albums, Songs) for a top list, `Noxio_Lil-Peep_….png` for an artist, album or song, `Noxio_2026_….png`, `Noxio_2026-Songs_….png` and `Noxio_2026-Summary_….png` for the year in review. The names are unit tested; saving and sharing were not tried on a device.
- README: links to the changelog, roadmap and releases under the badges, the additions Auxio has decided not to add (sleep timer, tag editing), the basic scope of tag editing, and what was removed from Auxio and why.
- Wavy seek bar switch is back in Settings > Appearance (Wavy seek bar, on by default; off for less motion). It was removed with the other appearance switches. Only the wave while playing follows it; a switch stored by an older backup is honoured again. The setting and the migration are unit tested; the switch was not tried on a device.
- Changelog in the app: About has a Changelog row that opens every release of `CHANGELOG.md`, newest first, one card per release with its sections (the file is copied into the app's assets at build time, so it is offline and always matches the installed version; its text stays English). The reading of the file is unit tested, including that every release in the real file reads; the screen was looked at on an emulator, not on a phone.
- Backup safeguard: a new backup is compared with the biggest of the saved backups (most plays, most playlists), not the newest one, so a slow decline cannot walk the saved backups down until the good ones are gone. Nothing is written when it has no plays and no playlists although the biggest had some, or when it has a quarter or more fewer plays (histories under 10 plays are not compared). A manual backup asks first; a scheduled one is skipped with a notification, and Settings > Backup asks the same question as it opens, with both numbers. Confirming writes a plain backup and makes that the level new backups are compared with (backups from before go once five newer ones exist). The `_small` files and the "always keep three good ones" rule of the earlier safeguard are gone; old `_small` files count as any other. The rules, the confirmation, the slow decline and the notification are unit tested; the question was looked at on an emulator, a scheduled skipped backup was not.
- Restoring a backup applies the theme, black theme and accent right away (the screen is made again), and switches the launcher icon if the backup has another one; before, they only showed after a restart. The detection is unit tested; the restore itself was not tried on a device.
- Edit tags: the keyboard opens with the first field focused. The earlier attempt did not work because an AlertDialog marks its window as not for typing when it opens without text fields, which the read-only properties are not; the flag is cleared now. Looked at on an emulator.
- Merged eight commits of upstream Auxio (reformat with trailing commas, queue adapter fix, song date sort by the song's own date then its album, doc path crash fix in musikr, dependency bumps). The `media` submodule stays at the last commit that builds: upstream's newest one removed a Media3 field but left a reference in `ProgressiveMediaSource.java`. Upstream's new wave check was inverted (it turned the wave off when animations are on) and is fixed here with a test, and sent upstream as a pull request together with the date popup crash fix (a date was passed to a number format; found by lint).
- Calculated ReplayGain removed because scanning the library in the app was too slow. Playback still uses ReplayGain tags already in music files; the scan, measured-value storage, tag-writing action, detail rows and backup inclusion are gone. Older backup ZIPs still restore their settings, history and playlists; their measured-gain entry is ignored.
- Album pages: the songs start right under the header, also on short albums (not at the bottom of the page). An album with one song has no sort button and no floating shuffle button. Tried on an emulator; screens have no tests.
- Tag editing: Song properties and Edit tags show title, artists, album, album artists, track and date in the same order; disc remains in the underlying metadata but is no longer shown or edited there. Both reading artist tags and Edit tags use the selected separator (default ;). Edits write native multi-value tags. Writing goes through TagLib, and Android asks for access first. Playlists follow songs whose UID changes after an edit. The tag edit rules and UID migration are unit tested; the dialogs were tried on an emulator.
- Fixed: on an album with one song (and other very short lists under a big header) the song was pushed below the screen, so it could not be tapped. Measuring the list counted the gap above it before the gap was applied, and the list swung between two places. Tried on an emulator; the list views have no tests.
- Queue before the library (Auxio #1467): every queued song's file record is saved with the queue (Room schema 38 to 39), and at start the song that played is built from the tag cache alone (a small public entry point in `musikr/`, `Preview`), so it is in the play strip and can play within a second or two instead of after the whole library. Only that song, because building a long queue takes as long as loading the library; when the library is there, the rest of the queue is added around the playing song without stopping it, and the saved queue is not overwritten before that. Songs reloaded with the library are also swapped into the player in place now instead of starting over. Saving and reading back and the queue rules are unit tested; tried on an emulator (5,372 songs: the song after about 1.4 s, the library after about 7 s, played through without a gap).
- Settings: groups that are not self-explanatory have an "i" in their header (ReplayGain strategy, Keyboard, Library > Music, Library > Content, Backup) that opens a short explanation, so the rows keep one-line summaries. Backup is split into Automatic backup (folder first, then schedule, day, time) and Manual (Back up now, Restore), and Launcher comes before Keyboard in Appearance. Screens and dialogs have no tests; tried on the emulator only.
- Settings: the ReplayGain pre-amp is removed (always 0 dB, also with an old saved value or a restored backup).
- Stats: a long title in a top list wraps to a second line before it is cut off, so albums that differ only at the end can be told apart The share cards and the card of one item (also used by the year in review) wrap long titles too, in a smaller size.
- Faster library loading: about 2.5 to 3 times faster than Auxio with the same result (emulator, 6,467 songs, 13.7 s to 5.4 s; Pixel 9 Pro, 476 songs, about 7.5 s to 2.5 s). The transliterator and its availability check are looked up once instead of for every name, names are remembered for one load, and progress is reported at most every 100 ms. Names and sorting were checked on a phone.
- Queue: swiping up on the open player opens the queue (swiping down closes the player). Only where the queue is a sheet below the player; on wide screens the queue is already beside it.
- Playlists and queue (from the open issues of Auxio): a song's menu on a playlist page has Remove from playlist (the first copy, if it is there twice); the menu of a playlist has Remove duplicates (greyed out when there are none); adding songs to a playlist skips those that are in it already and says so; the queue has a More button (Ctrl+M with the queue open) with Save as playlist, which opens the playlist picker with a new playlist at its end, and Clear queue, which empties the queue and stops playing; opening the queue always starts it at the song that plays.
- Playback state: the position is saved every 15 seconds while playing and whenever playback starts or stops, so the last song and its position survive the app being stopped without notice (Auxio issues about the last song not being remembered).
- Search: the best matches come first (exact, starts with, a word starts with, contains, all the words in any order, file name last), the same matches as before otherwise, equally good ones by name.
- Keyboard phone mode: the Search and Menu keys of phones that have them work like Ctrl+F and Ctrl+M.
- Keyboard phone mode (Settings > Appearance > Keyboard phone: automatic when a hardware keyboard is found, on or off), made for the Clicks Communicator, which has no arrow keys: no search rows (home, pages, queue; they show while typing), no tabs (swipe, or Ctrl+comma and Ctrl+period for the previous and next tab), no floating buttons (shuffle is Ctrl+S, new or imported playlists are in the Ctrl+M menu of the Playlists tab) and no buttons on the play strip. Switched on without a hardware keyboard, the search row and the settings icon stays, so the settings can always be reached by touch. Letters or digits (also those printed on letter keys, with Alt) search, or filter an artist or playlist page or the queue; Space plays or pauses; Backspace goes back without ever leaving the app; Ctrl+N/B next/previous, Ctrl+J/L 10 seconds back/ahead, Ctrl+P player, Ctrl+Q queue, Ctrl+S shuffle, Ctrl+R repeat, Ctrl+T sleep timer, Ctrl+F search, Ctrl+O sort, Ctrl+M menu, Ctrl+H or ? the overview of the keys, which also shows once by itself and in the settings. In the search Enter opens the first result. Checked on an emulator with its keyboard; on the Communicator itself pending, as is customizing the keys.
- Playing strip under the search row: on the home screen between the search row and the tabs, on album, artist and playlist pages and in the queue at the bottom, so it is nearer the thumb; the search row follows it and stays above the keyboard. The search field of a playlist page now shows the playlist name on first open.
- Short and square screens (under 480 dp of height, like the 1080 × 1200 Clicks Communicator): home tabs show icons only (labels kept for screen readers), the stats totals move into the bottom title row so the top list shows more rows, album, artist, genre and playlist headers lose the empty toolbar gap, and the player text is centered. Checked on an emulator at 1080 × 1200, 420 dpi; normal phones unchanged.
- Compact player: centered square artwork above centered song details and a shorter full-width control row, with the seek bar retained on screens down to 300 dp available height. The redundant top heading is hidden on these short screens to leave room for the cover. Checked at a Communicator-like 1080 × 1200 resolution at 400 and 480 dpi; real-phone testing pending.
- Sleep timer duration tiles: a tap selects a duration, a tap on the selected one edits it (switching between durations no longer opens the editor); they remember their values, and sort shortest to longest after confirmation; readable input and labels, with the active field kept above the keyboard. Defaults are 30 / 45 / 60 minutes, and edits remain capped at 1–599 minutes.
- Settings: the main settings menu and Appearance, Playback, Library and Backup place short preference lists at the bottom above the navigation inset. A small fixed title bar sits below the options, matching About; the top toolbar is removed. Long lists start at the top and scroll above the title bar.
- Library settings: Automatic reloading and the old all-tags Multi-value separators removed. Background watching is always off, with Refresh and Rescan still available; native multi-value tags stay supported. Artist separator is first in Content; it applies only to artist and album artist names stored as one tag, and to Edit tags. It defaults to semicolon, with ampersand, slash, plus and comma as alternatives. Ampersand splits "Lil Peep & Lil Tray", but also names such as "Simon & Garfunkel". Hide song-only artists follows it, then Hide secondary album artists. The latter groups albums and songs under their first album artist, including artist detail lists, and also hides song-only artists. The Content info dialog explains the distinction.
- Album and artist detail pages place artwork at the top and start short lists right under the header; long names scroll to show their full text.
- Music sources: Include hidden files removed; hidden files are always excluded in File picker mode, even with an old saved preference or restored backup. Other source and folder options are kept.
- Appearance: round mode is always enabled (the wavy seek bar has a switch again, see below); the Round mode and Force square album covers switches and the Album covers selector are removed. Cover proportions are preserved, including for old installs or restored settings. Artwork always uses Balanced quality (embedded artwork cached as JPEG quality 85 with a 750-pixel downsampling target); old quality choices are discarded, and the empty Images section is removed.
- Shorter item menus: duplicate Play, Shuffle and View entries removed; queue actions first, playlist management grouped with Delete last; audio sharing kept for songs
- Year in review ("Wrapped"): five cards to swipe through, share or save, with an optional name, a yearly listening heatmap and monthly listening time chart, from the menu of the listening stats
- Listening stats: top artists, albums and songs with plays and time per week, month, year or all time, tapping the shown period opens a picker shaded by listening time (ISO week numbers, months under their year, or years; oldest first and the shown one bright), the arrows stop at the first period with plays, swiping between artists, albums and songs, a closer look at a single artist, album or song, and colorful cards to share or save as an image
- Musicolet weekly and monthly "Most played songs" files import into their week or month, spread over its days (weekly preferred, a re-import replaces); imported plays count as estimated: for weeks listened, month and year bars and the first and last play (with ≈), not for days listened or day bars
- CSV export and import of the full listening history, with Musicolet-compatible columns
- Full backup of settings, listening history and playlists as Noxio_Backup_YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS.zip (day only before 1.24, noxio_ before 1.21), with scheduled backups, safeguards against much smaller backups and restore. Older backups with measured ReplayGain still open; that entry is ignored.
- Sleep timer with fade-out, finishing the last song and shake to restart
- Settings in four screens (Appearance with the library tabs, Playback, Library, Backup); week start, what plays from the library or a detail page, headset autoplay, pause on repeat, remember pause, always exit on close, intelligent sorting and the custom playback bar action removed
- Bottom navigation: home screen with the search row, tabs and floating shuffle button at the bottom; album, genre, playlist and artist pages with the same bottom row; search with filter chips at the bottom; no back buttons, gestures only
- Queue: search row under the playing strip, sort of the whole queue (name, artist, album, date, duration, random); now playing title centered
- Playback bar with previous, play and next; wavy seek bar while playing
- Default tab order Artists, Albums, Songs, Playlists, no Genres tab; no three-dot buttons on list rows (long press opens the item menu); black theme for menus, dialogs, sheets and bars
- Icon: Crescent and Sparkle, and a minimal white outline icon switchable in Settings > Appearance
- About page with Noxio links, the Auxio credit and fork notice; store listing (fastlane) with a Noxio description in English and German, new screenshots and feature graphic, Auxio's changelogs and other languages removed
- README screenshots of what differs from Auxio, retaken for the backup, the stats with imported plays and the sleep timer in the play controls
- German translation of everything Noxio added
- Unit tests for the Noxio logic, coverage badge, CI (format, Android lint, tests), build caching, signed releases with an ARM64 APK
