Noxio is a local music player for Android, built for listening through the night. It is a fork of Auxio and keeps its fast, reliable playback, and adds what a player you fall asleep to needs, with everything in reach of one hand.

<b>What Noxio adds</b>

- Sleep timer: hold play/pause, pick 30 min, 60 min or your own time, finish the last song, fade out, shake the phone to restart it. The countdown shows in the play button and in a quiet notification with +10 min and Stop
- Listening stats: top artists, albums and songs per week, month, year or all time, a closer look at each one, and colorful cards to share or save as an image
- Import your play counts from Musicolet, weekly and monthly lists included
- Backup of settings, listening history and playlists on a schedule you pick, with a safeguard against an empty backup replacing a good one, and restore
- One-handed layout: search, sort and tabs at the bottom, a floating shuffle button, and gestures instead of back buttons
- Queue with search and sort
- Pure black theme, a wavy seek bar you can turn off, and a minimal white outline icon
- Short settings in four screens

<b>From Auxio</b>

- Playback based on Media3 ExoPlayer, gapless, with full ReplayGain support
- Support for disc numbers, multiple artists, release types, precise dates, sort tags and more
- Reliable playlists, playback state persistence, Android Auto, widgets
- External equalizer support
- Completely private and offline

Noxio only reads your music and its tags and never edits them. It is a personal project, not affiliated with the Auxio project.
