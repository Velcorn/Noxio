# Getting around Noxio

Noxio has no back buttons and no three-dot buttons. Everything is a tap, a long press or a swipe, mostly
near the bottom of the screen. This page lists them all.

## Everywhere

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Back gesture | anywhere | goes back (closes the player, the queue, a page or a dialog) |
| Tap | a song, album, artist or playlist | plays the song, or opens the page |
| Long press | a row in any list | its menu: play next, add to queue or a playlist, go to the artist or album, … |
| Swipe left or right | the home lists | the next or previous tab |

## Home

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | a tab icon at the bottom | that list: artists, albums, songs, playlists, or Settings last |
| Tap | the floating search button | searches the whole library |
| Long press | the floating search button | a Shuffle all button pops up above it; a tap elsewhere closes it |
| Tap | the + button on Playlists | a new playlist |

## Album, artist and playlist pages

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | the floating search button | album: searches the library; artist or playlist: a filter field above the playing strip |
| Long press | the page header (name and details) or the search button | the page's menu: Shuffle, play next, add to queue or a playlist, Sort, … |
| Swipe left or right | an artist page | between its albums and its songs |

The artist menu in the Artists tab also has **Choose picture**: one of their album covers instead of the
collage.

## Settings tab

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | a row | opens that screen of Settings |
| Tap | the floating search button | searches every setting; tap a match to open its screen |

## Playing strip and player

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | the playing strip | opens the player |
| Long press | the playing strip | the song's menu |
| Long press | the song, artist or album in the player | the song's menu, with Go to artist and Go to album |
| Swipe left or right | the cover | the next or previous song |
| Swipe up | the player | opens the queue |
| Hold | play/pause | the sleep timer |

## Queue

| Do | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | a song | plays it |
| Drag | the handle at the end of a row | moves the song |
| Long press | a song | Remove from queue, add to a playlist, go to the artist or album, properties |
| Tap | the floating search button | searches the queue |
| Long press | the floating search button | Shuffle queue, Save as playlist, Clear queue |

## Sleep timer

Hold play/pause to open it. Tap a duration to pick it, tap the picked one again to change it. A running
timer shows in the play button and in a notification with +10 min, Restart and Stop; shaking the phone
restarts it.

## Keyboard phones

With a hardware keyboard (automatic, or switched on in Appearance), typing searches, Space plays and pauses,
and Ctrl with a letter does the rest. Ctrl+H lists all keys.
