Listening stats

A barebones, always-on Wrapped. Pick a period at the top and a category in the pill at the bottom. Both are within thumb reach, and every card can be shared as an image.

Cards and sharing

  • Each category has its own colors. The card on screen and the shared image look the same.
  • The share icon on a card makes a portrait image with the period, the top 5, plays and time, and the total.
  • Share sends it to any app. Save image keeps it on the device.
  • The rest of the top 10 sits under the card.

What counts

  • A song counts as a play after 30 seconds, or half of it if it is under a minute.
  • Only time spent playing counts. Pausing adds nothing, and skipping ahead does not add time.
  • Plays are stored on the device only.

Export

  • CSV export of the play history is planned. The format is still open, see the project roadmap.
