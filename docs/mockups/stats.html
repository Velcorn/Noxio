Listening stats

A barebones listening summary, like a small Wrapped that is always on. It lives in the Home overflow menu.

What counts

  • A song counts as a play after 30 seconds of listening, or half of it if it is under a minute.
  • Only time spent playing counts. Pausing adds nothing, and skipping ahead in a song does not add time.
  • Plays are stored on the device only, in their own database.

Periods

  • Week, month, year: calendar periods. The week starts on the first day of the week of your locale.
  • Step back to earlier periods. The next arrow is disabled on the current one.
  • Top 10 songs, artists and albums, ranked by plays and then by time listened.

Not yet

  • Live updates while the screen is open, export, and deleting the history.
  • A shareable summary card.
