Noxio navigation mockups

Second round. Everything is plain black with small icons and text, and the controls sit at the bottom. Tabs, menus, sheets, search and sorting are clickable. Example data only.

1. Home screen

Your pick, "everything at the bottom", without any bars or pills. There is no top bar at all: the list starts under the status bar. From the bottom up: the five destinations, the search row (search, sort and the overflow menu with Listening stats, Settings and About), and the mini player with a thin progress line. Two ways to draw the destinations.

2. Playlist

Close to the current screen: cover, "Playlist", name and info on top, then the song list. Changes: the top bar is gone. Back, play, shuffle and the overflow menu are in a bar at the bottom, in the thumb zone. The pencil is gone: the sort button directly opens the sort sheet, and "Custom order" in that sheet lets you drag songs (with Cancel and Save at the bottom). The list header also has the search for this playlist. The overflow menu keeps today's items (Play next, Add to queue, Rename, Delete, Share) and adds Export as M3U.

3. About page

Renamed to Noxio, with the back button at the bottom like the rest. The Auxio credit stays, as the license requires, with a notice that Noxio is a fork and upstream is not responsible for it.

ReplayGain (later)

Calculating ReplayGain means decoding every song and measuring its loudness (EBU R128). Android has no built-in way to do that, so it needs a loudness library. It would run as a background job with a progress notification and store the values in Noxio's own database, not in your files. It works, but it is the largest item on the list, so I would do it last and as its own step. The menu item is in the mockups so you can see where it would live.
