Detail pages mockups

The controls of an artist page go to the very bottom, and a second row switches between Albums and Songs. Each of the two keeps its own sort, and remembers it. Everything is clickable: switch the tabs, open the sort sheet, change a sort, switch away and back.

1. Artist page

Today the artist page is one long list: albums, then songs, with the controls in a bar at the top. Now the name is part of the page, the controls are at the bottom like on the playlist page, and Albums and Songs are two tabs, each with a sort button in its list header. The header also names the sort in use, so you can see what each tab is set to. Three ways to lay out the bottom. B has two ways to mark the tab you are on.

2. Album and genre pages

The same bar, without tabs, since there is only one list. Your pick for the artist page decides how the bar looks here: this shows the bar of proposal A (back, name, play, shuffle, more). The sort stays in the header of the song list.
