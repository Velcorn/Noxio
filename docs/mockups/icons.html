Noxio icon concepts

Dark and moody, a moon and a play button. Every concept has a pure black background for AMOLED screens, and a single-color version for Android's themed icons, which Material You tints with your wallpaper colors.

Shape

How it maps to the app. Android icons are two layers on a 108 dp grid: a background and a foreground, plus an optional monochrome layer that the launcher tints on Android 13 and later. Noxio already has all three slots, so a chosen concept is just new artwork in those files. The launcher mask (circle, squircle, and so on) is the user's choice, hence the shape buttons.
