Icon tips

Each card shows the icon at 144, 96, 64 and 48 px, the sizes of launcher grids and pickers. The crescent of the current icon tapers to a hairline at both tips. The other cards round the tips off a little more each time, so there is nothing thinner than a few pixels left to lose. Look at the 48 and 64 px sizes: that is where thin ends vanish.

Left to right in each card: the default icon is the lavender one on black, the minimal icon is the white one without a background.
