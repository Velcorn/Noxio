Settings structure

Today the settings are spread over four screens plus three loose rows at the top level. "Content" mixes tags, covers and listening stats, "Personalize" mixes tabs, the app icon and playback behaviour, and the library actions (Refresh, Rescan) sit outside the Library screen. Below is the current layout and three ways to regroup it.

Now

A. By purpose (five screens)

Everything about looks in one place, everything about playing in another. The library actions move into the Library screen, so the top level has only screens.

B. Four screens

Fewer screens, longer lists with headings. Sound and playback are one screen, listening stats live in the library.

C. Smallest change

Keep the four screens, but move the odd ones: library actions into Content, the app icon next to the other looks, no new screens.
