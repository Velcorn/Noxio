Sleep timer, built for one hand

The timer is a small moon icon in an action row above the seek bar, like in Musicolet. The play button and the other controls sit at the very bottom, where a thumb rests.

Reach

  • Bottom half: the action row, seek bar, playback controls, and every control in the sheet.
  • Top: only the close arrow and the title. Swiping down also closes the screen.
  • Turn on Show thumb zone under the phone to see the lower half highlighted.

Behavior

  • Off: a plain moon icon.
  • Running: the icon turns accent colored and shows the time left, or "End". Tapping it opens the sheet.
  • Choices: End of track, 30 min, Custom (tap to type any number of minutes, tap again to change it) and 60 min. The last choice is preselected next time, and End of track is the first-run default.
  • Fade out: volume drops over the last 8 seconds, then playback pauses. On by default.
  • Reset: a notification action and shaking the device both restart a running minutes timer. Use Simulate shake to try it here.

Notification and themes

  • While a minutes timer runs, "Reset N min timer" takes the shuffle slot in the notification, because Android's media notification has no room for a sixth button.
  • Try the theme and color menus above the phone. AMOLED black uses true black surfaces, and a Material You color follows your wallpaper. The app has both in Settings > Personalize.

Not in the mockup

  • A bottom bar for jumping from the player to Songs, Albums, Artists and Playlists. Deferred until after the timer and stats.
  • The equalizer shortcut lives in Settings > Audio, since the timer took its place.
