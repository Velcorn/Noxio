Black menus, seek bar and notification icons

With the black theme every menu, dialog and sheet becomes pure black, so it needs something to set it apart from the black screen behind it. The cards below show the menu, the dialog and the bottom sheet in the current dark grey and in two black variants.

Menus, dialogs and sheets

A is the one that is built: pure black with a 1 dp border of #2E2E2E. B has a lighter border (#444444) that stands out more on a bright screen.

Seek bar

The seek bar of the player waves while a song plays. A new switch, Settings, UI, "Wavy seek bar", decides. It is off by default, which shows the plain line below, and on gives the wave as before. The thin bar of the mini player is a plain line already.

On: waves while playing
Off (default): plain coloured line

Notification

The notification cannot tint icons, so the on state of shuffle and repeat could only be shown with a dot. Now the off state is the same icon faded to grey, the on state is the icon at full strength, and repeat one keeps its 1 in the arrows.
